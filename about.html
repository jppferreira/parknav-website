About

Built for the last ten minutes of the journey.

ParkNav exists because arriving shouldn’t mean another loop of the block. We help you see where parking looks possible — then you confirm it on the street.

Why ParkNav

A companion, not a booking app.

Most parking products sell a space. ParkNav starts with what’s already on the street: free bays, conditions, paid backups, airport and long-stay options. The data comes from OpenStreetMap — the same open map that powers a large part of the world’s navigation — plus optional sign photos when you arrive.

That means coverage varies. Tags can be incomplete. A green street on your phone can still be full. We say that in the app, and we say it here. The job is to narrow the search so you spend less time guessing.

How we work

A few things we won’t pretend.

We don’t sell your data

Location, searches, and optional sign photos are used to run the feature you asked for. We don’t sell personal data. App Store and Google Play handle payments, so card numbers never sit with us.

We don’t replace the sign

ParkNav is not legal advice, enforcement, or a reservation. You are responsible for checking signs, paying fees, and following local rules.

We credit the map

Parking locations and many restriction tags come from OpenStreetMap contributors, used under the Open Database License. See Data & open sources.
