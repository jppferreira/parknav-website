OpenStreetMap

Parking locations, streets, and many restriction tags come from OpenStreetMap. © OpenStreetMap contributors. Data available under the Open Database License (ODbL). You may use OSM data under those licence terms; ParkNav’s own UI and analysis are separate.

More: openstreetmap.org/copyright

Geocoding & parking queries

Destination lookups use Nominatim in Germany. Nearby parking is queried from Overpass servers in France and Germany. Please use ParkNav responsibly — do not hammer public OSM infrastructure with excessive automated requests.

Sign reading (optional)

When you photograph a sign, text may be sent to OCR.space for recognition. Do not photograph people or private information unnecessarily. Sign reading is heuristic and may be wrong.

Payments

On App Store and Google Play builds, in-app purchases and subscriptions are billed by Apple or Google only. Renewals, refunds, and cancellation follow their policies. Manage subscriptions in your device account settings or via Plan → Manage in the app.
