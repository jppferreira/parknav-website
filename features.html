Product

Everything in the app, in one place.

ParkNav is a parking companion — search, arrive, check the sign, and get back on time.

Search

Near you, or wherever you’re heading next.

Use device location, or type a destination. ParkNav geocodes the place, then looks at streets within walking distance — 5 to 45 minutes, you choose.

Plan ahead

Today through next week, 30 minutes to 14 days.

Set arrival time and stay length. We check whether mapped hours and max-stay tags still look open when you plan to be there.

Nearby, airport, long stay

Three trip modes, one habit.

Nearby puts free street parking first. Airport and long stay lean on operator and long-stay tags, so a weekend away or a flight doesn’t use the same ranking as a coffee stop.

Results

List or map. Best, closest, cheapest, longest.

Each street shows status — free, free with conditions, restricted, paid — plus walking time and confidence. Sort the list, or switch to the map and tap a bay.

Signs

Evidence from the map, and a camera when you arrive.

Where OpenStreetMap has restriction text, we show it in plain English. Check sign lets you photograph the plate for a second reading. Heuristic — the metal sign still wins.

Plan B, timer, alerts

A backup street, a session clock, a way out if it’s full.

If the first street looks busy, ParkNav keeps Plan B and Plan C. Start a timer when you park. Optional reminders before you need to move. Community notes can flag a street as full or freeing up — treat them as hints, not guarantees.

Also in the app

Filters that match how you actually drive.

Disabled parking, resident permit, vehicle type (car, motorcycle, van, EV), and a free-only toggle. Directions open in Apple Maps, Google Maps, or Waze. Paid plans can save a location and share a recommendation.
