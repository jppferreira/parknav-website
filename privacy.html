Privacy Policy

Last updated 23 October 2026 · v1.0.0

Who we are

ParkNav is a mobile parking companion operated by J. P. Ferreira in the United Kingdom. J. P. Ferreira is the controller for personal data described on this page. For privacy questions or to use your rights, use the contact form.

What ParkNav does

ParkNav helps you find parking near a destination using map data and parking guidance. It is not a booking service, enforcement tool, or legal advice. You must always check roadside signs and local rules. Sign reading is guidance only. It does not decide whether you may park.

Information we process

  • Location — only while the app is open and you have allowed it. We do not collect location in the background. It is used to search for parking near you.
  • Destination searches — the place you type is sent to Nominatim in Germany to find coordinates. Nearby parking is then requested from Overpass servers in France or Germany.
  • Device identifier — a random ID stored on your phone to apply trial and pass limits. It stays on the phone. Restoring a purchase asks Apple or Google for purchases already on that store account. The random ID is not sent with that request.
  • Optional photos — Check sign can use the camera, or you can choose one photo with the system photo picker. ParkNav does not ask for your photo library. On this release the photo is read on the iPhone and is not uploaded. We do not use photos for advertising, and ParkNav does not keep the photo after the check.
  • Contact form — your name, email, and message are sent to Web3Forms in the United States so we can reply.
  • Crash reports — an error is kept on your phone. It is sent only if you tap Send a report, or if you attach it to the contact form. A report you send goes to Web3Forms in the United States so we can fix the app. It does not include your name or email. If crash reporting is later switched on, the same report is also sent to Sentry in the United States.
  • Payment status — App Store and Google Play purchases are handled by Apple or Google only. We receive purchase status, not your card number.
  • Notifications — reminders are scheduled on your phone. They are not sent to us.
  • This website — loading the typefaces sends your IP address to Google in the United States.

Why we use this information

Each purpose has one UK GDPR basis. A phone permission prompt is a device control. It is not UK GDPR consent, and we do not rely on consent for the items below.

  • Parking search, sign check, reminders, and your pass — contract, because this is the service you asked for.
  • Applying plan limits and restoring a purchase — contract. Our legitimate interest is stopping those limits from being bypassed.
  • Replying to a contact message — legitimate interests in answering you. A legal-rights request is handled because the law requires it.
  • A crash report you choose to send — legitimate interests in fixing the app. The same interest covers an automatic report if crash reporting is switched on.
  • Showing this website in its typefaces — legitimate interests in presenting the site. The interest is limited to loading the fonts, and you can read the page text without them.

Where data goes

Some providers are outside the United Kingdom.

  • Germany and France (Nominatim and Overpass) — covered by the UK adequacy regulations for the European Economic Area.
  • United States (Web3Forms for a contact message or a crash report you choose to send, Google Fonts, and Apple or Google for purchases) — sent under the UK International Data Transfer Addendum, or the UK-US data bridge where that provider is certified. If crash reporting is switched on, Sentry in the United States receives those reports under the same terms.

We do not send parking searches to Russia. We do not sell personal data. Each provider has its own privacy policy.

Storage and retention

  • Data on your phone, including the device ID, plan, and preferences, stays there until you delete the app or its data.
  • ParkNav does not keep a sign photo after the check. On this release the words are read on the iPhone, and the photo is not uploaded.
  • Contact messages and crash reports you choose to send are kept for up to 24 months so we can deal with the enquiry and any follow-up, then deleted. We keep a message longer only when the law requires it.
  • Purchase status we receive from Apple or Google is kept for up to 6 years for tax and disputes, then deleted or anonymised.

Your rights

If you are in the United Kingdom, you can ask us to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you
  • correct it
  • delete it
  • restrict how we use it
  • provide a copy you can take to another service (portability)
  • object to processing based on legitimate interests

Use the contact form. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office. You can switch off location, camera, or notifications in your device settings. That stops further collection of those items. It is separate from the rights above.

Children

ParkNav is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has used the contact form, tell us through that form and we will delete the message.

Changes

When this notice changes, the “Last updated” date changes with it. If a change affects how we use personal data, the updated notice will be published on this page and in the app before we rely on it.
