Privacy Policy

Last updated 15 October 2026 · v1.0.0

Who we are

ParkNav (“we”, “us”) provides a mobile parking companion app. For privacy questions, contact support@parknav.app.

What ParkNav does

ParkNav helps you find parking near a destination using map data and parking guidance. It is not a booking service, enforcement tool, or legal advice. You must always check roadside signs and local rules.

Information we process

  • Location — when you allow it, we use your device GPS to search parking near you.
  • Destination searches — place names or addresses you enter are sent to geocoding services to find coordinates.
  • Device identifier — a random ID stored on your device to manage your plan and search limits.
  • Optional photos — if you use Check sign, a photo may be sent to our OCR provider to read sign text. We do not use photos for advertising.
  • Payment data — purchases on iPhone and Android are handled by Apple App Store or Google Play. We receive purchase status, not your full card number.
  • Notifications — if enabled, we schedule local reminders for your parking session on your device.

Why we use this information

We process data to provide parking search, sign checking, session reminders, subscription or pass access, fraud prevention, and to improve reliability. Legal bases (UK GDPR): contract (providing the service you requested), legitimate interests (security and product improvement), and consent where required (e.g. location, camera, notifications).

Third-party services

ParkNav relies on external services that may process data on our behalf:

  • OpenStreetMap / Nominatim / Overpass — map and parking data
  • OCR.space — optional sign text recognition from photos
  • Apple App Store / Google Play — in-app purchases and subscriptions on mobile
  • Stripe — optional payments for non-store test builds only
  • Apple / Google — app distribution and device permissions

Each provider has its own privacy policy. We choose services appropriate for the feature and do not sell your personal data.

Storage and retention

Most preferences and plan information are stored on your device. Server-side billing records (if you purchase a pass or subscription) are kept as long as needed for payments, accounting, and dispute handling, then deleted or anonymised when no longer required.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict use of your personal data, and to object to certain processing. To exercise these rights, email support@parknav.app. You may also complain to the ICO (UK) or your local data protection authority.

Children

ParkNav is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes

We may update this policy. The “Last updated” date will change when we do. Continued use after changes means you accept the updated policy.
