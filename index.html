How it works

Three quiet steps. Then you’re parked.

01

Tell us where you’re going

Use your current location, or type a street, station, or airport. Search for today, or pick a date later this week.

02

We look along nearby streets

ParkNav reads OpenStreetMap parking data — free bays first, then paid, airport, and long-stay options if you need them.

03

Drive to the best option

Open the map, walk the last minutes, and keep Plan B ready if the street is full.

Free first

We look for free parking before we show you anything else.

Nearby mode ranks free and free-with-conditions streets first. Paid options stay in reserve so you’re never left circling with no plan.

Trip modes

The same search, tuned to the trip.

Check the sign

Photograph the plate. We’ll help you read it.

Rules change. Map tags miss things. When you arrive, point the camera at the sign so you can see hours, max stay, and conditions in plain English — then always trust the street over the app.

While you’re away

A timer, and a nudge before you need to move.

Start a session when you leave the car. ParkNav can remind you before the stay ends — so a free bay doesn’t become a ticket.
