Questions

How billing works

Do I need a pass to search?

The trial includes 8 searches and 2 sign checks over 3 days. After that, or when those run out, pick a pass to keep searching.

What does monthly unlock?

10 searches and 8 sign checks a day, a wider radius, and saving and sharing. Yearly includes the same daily limits for £39.99 a year.

How do I cancel?

Monthly and yearly renew until you cancel in your Apple or Google account settings. The 24-hour pass does not renew.

Is parking guaranteed?

No. ParkNav is guidance from maps and optional sign reading. Bays fill up. Always check signs before you leave the car.
