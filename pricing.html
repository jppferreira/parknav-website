Questions

How billing works

Do I need a pass to search?

No. The free plan includes unlimited parking searches near you or a typed destination.

What does monthly unlock?

Saving locations, sharing a recommendation, searching further afield, and the other extras described in the app at purchase.

How do I cancel?

If you subscribed through Apple or Google, cancel in that store. If you paid with Stripe, use Manage Subscription in the app Plan screen.

Is parking guaranteed?

No. ParkNav is guidance from maps and optional sign reading. Bays fill up. Always check signs before you leave the car.
